==================================================
🔐 CORE VPN ENGINE (WAJIB SEMUA)
==================================================

Implementasikan dan integrasikan SEMUA protokol berikut:

### Modern & Anti-Blokir
- Xray Core (latest stable)
  - VLESS (TLS, TCP, WS, GRPC, XTLS Vision)
  - VMess
  - Trojan
  - Shadowsocks (AEAD)
  - HTTP/2
  - QUIC (jika memungkinkan)

### Classic / Legacy
- WireGuard
- OpenVPN (TCP & UDP)
- L2TP/IPSec
- PPTP (opsional, warning insecure)

### Tambahan
- SOCKS5 Proxy
- HTTP Proxy

Semua protokol:
- Bisa hidup berdampingan
- Multi-port
- Multi-inbound
- Bisa enable/disable per protokol
